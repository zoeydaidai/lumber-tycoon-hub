Lumber Tycoon secret badge: start here

Most searches for “Lumber Tycoon secret badge” now refer to the hidden Lumber Tycoon 2 route in The Hunt: Roblox 20. It is separate from the normal wood-delivery objective, and it has changed since early walkthroughs appeared.

Updated September 28, 2026About 6 minutes to readIndependent player guide

Quick answer

Finish the regular Lumber Tycoon 2 Hunt objective first, then complete the hidden cave-and-item route for the secret badge. The key checkpoints are the Skeleton permission, the orange globe/item mechanism, the purple room items, and the bunker delivery. Use the exact route pages below instead of following old pre-update instructions.

Why there are two “badge” searches

The normal Hunt task asks players to deliver wood until the event progress is complete. The secret badge is a separate chain unlocked after that route. Search results call it a secret badge, secret quest, or the Glorb route—so this hub keeps the phrasing together while giving each question its own answer.

Complete the normal objective

Make the wood deliveries register with the event NPC until the regular task confirms completion. Do not confuse a first hand-in with the finished quest.

Prepare for the cave

Bring an axe, a vehicle if possible, and a Work Light. The light is for the cave—not a cosmetic step—and a vehicle makes the multi-item return route safer.

Follow the current hidden route

Talk to the Skeleton, work through the special globe/item sequence, open the next area, and transport the three required objects to the bunker.

Confirm before leaving

Watch for the in-game award feedback before changing server. Secret-route requirements can be individual to the player and may not carry a whole party.

Important: This is an event-facing guide. If your server map or NPC dialogue differs, prioritize the current in-game prompt. Older guides may still show a blocked cave, planks, and dynamite steps that no longer apply to the updated route.
