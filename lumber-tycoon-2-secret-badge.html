Lumber Tycoon 2 secret badge guide

A current-path guide for the hidden Hunt 20 badge: what to bring, what now opens without the old entrance puzzle, and what to do when the next door will not open.

Updated September 28, 2026Allow 15–30 minutesBring a vehicle if you can

Before you start

Complete the regular Geck / wood-delivery event objective on the slot you are using. Bring an axe, a Work Light, and a truck or car if available. The light helps in the dark cave; the vehicle is for carrying several items later in the route.

Current secret badge route

Enter the cave and speak with the Skeleton

Use the current cave entrance and descend the installed truss/ladder route. Find the Skeleton and select the permission dialogue option. This is the gate that lets the hidden item sequence register.

Get the event bag/item from the main objective

Return to the regular event loop if you do not have the required Purple Bag/item yet. Keep the bag safe until the Skeleton interaction is complete.

Find the color-cycling globe and stop at orange

Open the event bags until you have the globe/item that cycles through colors. Interact until it reaches its final orange state, then stop. Some reports count 18 interactions, but watch the color—continuing can cycle past the target.

Use the correct lava-side mechanism

Carry the orange globe through the cave and place it in the right-side opening at the lava section. Wait for the next route to activate before moving on.

Collect and transport the three purple-room objects

Pick up all three items from the newly opened area. Put them in a vehicle together if possible; leaving one behind creates an unnecessary return trip.

Open the bunker and match the final items

Place the three objects in the bunker inputs. Inside, match the items from the boxes to the correct characters/plate: glass with the white Dominus character, the purple-bag item with the character without a Dominus, and the can on the gold Dominus plate.

If the door or badge does not trigger

  • Re-check that the normal event objective is actually marked complete, not merely started.
  • Make sure the Skeleton permission dialogue has been accepted before opening the bag/item.
  • Return to the globe and verify it is orange. Do not rely on an old click count if the visual state differs.
  • Use the right-side lava opening, then wait for the mechanism to react before collecting the next objects.
  • Do the route yourself if you are in a group; secret quest credit can be player-specific.
Do not buy TNT for the current route just because an old video says so. Updated walkthroughs report a truss entrance and no longer require the old plank-and-dynamite cave-opening sequence.
